Where a lead goes after someone taps your ad

  1. Ad click

    Meta and Google count the click. That is usually the last thing they hear. The click ID, campaign and ad are saved the moment the chat starts.

  2. WhatsApp reply

    Counted as a button tap. Nobody can tell which chats were real buyers. Qualified leads go back to Meta and Google, matched by a hashed phone number and counted once.

  3. Closed deal

    Lives in the CRM. The ad platforms never find out which ad produced it. The CRM stage is sent back as a conversion. You see cost per closed deal, by ad.

Result: the ads learn to find cheap taps, not buyers.

Where does your trail break?

Five questions about your own setup. Your answers stay in your browser.

When someone starts a WhatsApp chat from an ad, do you store the click ID, campaign and ad?

Without it, a chat can't be matched to the ad later.

Does your landing page fire a tracked event when someone taps the WhatsApp button?

If it doesn't, those leads are invisible in your ad account.

Are you on the WhatsApp Business Platform (API), not only the free app?

The free app can't send conversation data back to Meta.

Can your CRM name the ad behind a closed deal, without asking the agent?

Agent memory isn't attribution.

Do qualified leads or booked viewings get sent back to Meta and Google?

This is how the platforms learn what a buyer looks like.
